# OmniCare General Insurance Policy 2026

## Section 1: Home Water Damage Coverage

Water damage caused by sudden pipe bursts is covered up to $25,000 with a $500 deductible. Gradual leaks or flood damage are strictly excluded.

## Section 2: Personal Property Protection

Electronics, furniture, and jewelry are covered up to $10,000 total. Single items exceeding $2,500 require individual appraisal receipts.

## Section 3: Personal Liability Coverage

Bodily injury or property damage you cause to others is covered up to $300,000 per incident with no deductible. Liability arising from business activities conducted at the residence is excluded.

## Section 4: Theft and Burglary

Theft of insured belongings is covered up to $15,000 per incident with a $250 deductible. A police report filed within 72 hours of discovery is required for any theft claim.

## Section 5: Temporary Living Expenses

If the residence becomes uninhabitable after a covered loss, reasonable lodging and meal costs are reimbursed up to $8,000. Reimbursement is limited to 90 days from the date of the covered loss.

## Section 6: Filing Deadlines and Documentation

Claims must be reported within 30 days of the incident, and supporting receipts must be supplied within 60 days. Claims reported after 30 days may be denied unless the delay was unavoidable.

## Section 7: General Exclusions

Damage from earthquake, war, nuclear hazard, or intentional acts by the policyholder is excluded from every section of this policy. Wear, tear, and ordinary deterioration are never covered.
